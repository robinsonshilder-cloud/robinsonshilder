Eligibility & documents

The announcement welcomes children, youth and adults. It mentions the following registration documents:

  • Baptism certificate
  • RDTL document
  • Student identification card
  • Electoral card
  • Bilhete de Identidade (BI)

The exact accepted alternatives and age-specific requirements should be confirmed with T-LAC before visiting. Do not send sensitive identity documents through this demonstration website.

Frequently asked questions

When does registration open?

Registration is advertised to begin on 13 November 2026. Regular registration runs through 31 December 2026, followed by an extended period from 1 to 9 January 2027.

What is the monthly cost of a course?

The poster lists US$5, US$10, US$15 and US$20 per month, but does not identify which amount applies to each course. Contact T-LAC to confirm your course fee.

Can I study on weekends?

The poster lists a Saturday–Sunday half-time option (two days per week). Confirm availability for your particular course before registering.

Which documents do I need to register?

The poster mentions a baptism certificate, RDTL document, student ID, electoral card and BI. It does not clarify whether these are alternatives. Confirm the exact documents relevant to your application with T-LAC.

Can I register or pay through this website?

Not yet. This is an informational website. You can prepare an inquiry to copy and share with the Academy by your preferred channel.
